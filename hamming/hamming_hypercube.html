Hypercube of n-bit messages

Code blocks: 0 Unassigned vertices: 0
Every vertex is one of the possible bitstrings. An edge joins two vertices whose bitstrings differ in exactly one bit — a Hamming distance of 1. Hover a vertex to see its neighbors. The all-zero message and the single-1 vertices, along with the edges between them, are highlighted. Drag a single-1 vertex to shear the cube along that bit, or drag the zero vertex to translate the whole cube at once. Click any vertex to group it with its neighbors into a code block, shown in its own color — click any vertex in that block again to remove it. Blocks can't overlap, since that's the packing challenge. Each block's codeword and its one-error variations appear as a labeled column below the graph. Scroll to zoom in or out around the pointer (vertices and labels stay the same size); drag the background to pan; double-click to reset the view.
